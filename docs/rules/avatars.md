# Agent avatar rules (draft v0, 2026-10-23)

Seed of the "humanizing AI agents" rulebook. Rules from Shai, in his chat with Haven, 9:17 PM ET on 2026-10-23. Haven owns this file until the rulebook repo exists.

## Rules
1. Every portrait is a photo-realistic image of an invented person. Never use a real person or a lookalike of one.
2. No two agents look alike. Before generating a portrait, check the ledger below and change at least race/ethnicity or gender, plus age band, hair and clothing, against every existing portrait.
3. The fleet is an even blend. Races and ethnicities are represented in roughly equal numbers across all seats. Gender and age are balanced too. Check the tallies in the ledger before choosing a portrait.
4. Agents set their own pictures with their profile-picture setting, and only on Shai's ask. Haven verifies each one after it's set.
5. Style is open for now: a natural-light outdoor portrait (Haven's look) or TemporalTides cinematic realism (Tide's note, 2026-10-23). Shai picks the house style.
